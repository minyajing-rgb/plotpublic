# PLOT Meta cold-start test · 2026-10-04

This record covers **PLOT only**. Other products have separate campaigns, assets, destinations, and measurement. The first test uses English images for a US audience aged 22–55, with all genders and no Wi-Fi-only restriction. Its objective is landing-page views; the business question is whether visitors explore planned pricing and reserve free early access. A click or landing-page view alone does not establish willingness to pay.

The image ad uses [ten approved images](assets/production/meta-2026-10-03/) and the destination `https://plot.saga1001.com/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=plot_us_price_reservation_v2&utm_content=creative_mix_10`. Its CTA is **Book Now** and it describes a concept and early-access reservation, without promising live paid analysis or a charge today. Dynamic campaign, ad-set, and ad IDs were configured as Meta URL parameters. PLOT-specific Meta website conversion events are not yet verified, so platform reporting cannot currently establish completed reservations or purchases.

At the 2026-10-04 live check, the image ad showed **Learning** with its switch on. Ten images, five primary texts, five headlines, and five descriptions were visible in the editor. This is a point-in-time state, not proof of impressions, spend, reservations, or sales. The Facebook ad identity was **Luna Games**; a dedicated PLOT Page remains a future branding improvement.
