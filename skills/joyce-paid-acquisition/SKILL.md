# Joyce paid acquisition

Use this for each product independently. Record the product, audience, offer, landing page, budget, account, campaign, and success event before changing a live platform. Never reuse another product's pixel, identity, URL, creative, or conversion claim without verification.

## Defaults from Joyce

- English creative: prioritize the United States. Chinese creative: prioritize relevant Chinese-speaking audiences and Asia-Pacific markets, selecting exact countries from the product's availability and landing-page reachability. Keep ad text, on-image text, video spoken language, subtitles, landing page, and audience language aligned. Never place Chinese-only creative into an English-only test group, or vice versa.
- Default age 22–55 unless a product or audience requires a different range. Include all available connection types (Wi-Fi and mobile data, including 3G/4G/5G when selectable); do not accidentally set Wi-Fi-only delivery. Verify the platform's actual targeting controls rather than assuming they exist.
- Fill every meaningful available primary-text, headline, description, CTA, and asset slot with distinct, accurate variants, up to platform limits. Use all approved, quality-checked images that fit the test; do not fill slots with weak, repeated, wrong-language, outdated, or misleading material just to reach a count. Keep video as a separate test cell/ad group where budget can support interpretation, with Chinese video for Chinese audiences and English video for English audiences.
- For PLOT imagery, preserve `plot美术风格锚定`: ChatGPT/image editing for source visuals; Gaoding normally handles finished-image crop, dimensions, layout, and resolution, not replacement AI generation.
- Do not imply an unfinished feature, active payment flow, download, booking, or AI capability is already live. Label AI-generated/edited material as required by the ad platform; do not add unnecessary AI marketing claims.

## Execution boundary

Build and verify drafts and previews first. Before publishing or spending, show the exact platform preview or a clearly identified authenticated editor link, targeting, exclusions/expansion, objective, optimization event, attribution, identity, CTA, destination, schedule, currency, total and daily spend limits, and any unresolved measurement gaps. Obtain Joyce's explicit go-ahead for that concrete version. Publishing, approval/review, active delivery, conversions, and payment are distinct states; verify each before reporting it.

Use the [campaign scope and checklist](references/campaign-scope-checklist.md) for planning, launch QA, and reporting. Check current platform UI and official documentation for mutable settings and limits.
